Methodology & Assumptions

Simulation uses abstract skill-based probabilistic outcomes — no real poker hand logic is implemented. Base win probability: 0.5 − (opponentSkill − playerSkill) / scalingFactor, clamped to [5 %, 95 %]. Each format applies its own edge-realization and volatility modifiers. Poker Match Beginner additionally compresses skill edge by a configurable factor to reflect its simplified mechanics (redraw, fixed bets, best-of-three structure). Results are averages across Monte Carlo runs and should be interpreted directionally, not as precise predictions.
